# Future Ideas & Brainstorming

## AI/ML Enhancements
- **Multi-Genre Training**: Train models on specific genres (e.g., Psytrance, Eurobeat, Metal) to capture unique rhythmic patterns.
- **Visual Onset Detection**: Utilize the waveform visualization data directly in a CNN for potentially more accurate onset detection.
- **User-in-the-loop Training**: Allow users to "rate" or "correct" generated charts, feeding that data back into the models for fine-tuning.

## Porting & Architecture
- **Rust Port**: Consider porting core engine components to Rust for improved memory safety and performance.
- **Web-based Viewer**: Create a lightweight simfile viewer using WebAssembly to allow users to preview charts in their browser.

## Bobcoin & Economy
- **Tournament Pool**: A global pool of Bobcoin that is rewarded to winners of periodic community tournaments.
- **Decentralized Chart Hosting**: Store simfiles and audio on IPFS, with access managed by Bobcoin ownership or physical activity tiers.
